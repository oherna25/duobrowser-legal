DuoBrowser Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

Overview

DuoBrowser is a web browser for iPhone made by Osmar Hernandez-Bojorges. This policy describes what the app does with information on your device.

Information stored on your iPhone

Bookmarks you save, browsing history for pages you open outside private browsing (up to 500 visits), open tab addresses, and settings such as Tri-Screen mode, appearance, and Clear All Data on Exit stay on this iPhone. DuoBrowser does not send that information to the developer. Deleting the app removes it. Clear All Data on Exit, and Clear all browsing data now in Settings, remove history and website data.

Information the developer does not collect

DuoBrowser has no account, no advertising, and no analytics. The developer does not receive your browsing history, bookmarks, searches, location, contacts, or photos, and does not sell or share personal information.

Websites you visit

When you open a page, your device connects directly to that website. What the site does with your request is covered by that site's own policy. Pages are loaded with the system WebKit engine.

Search

If you type words into the address bar instead of a web address, DuoBrowser opens a DuckDuckGo search for those words. The query is sent to DuckDuckGo as a normal page request. DuoBrowser does not receive a copy of it.

Camera, microphone, and location

A website can ask to use the camera, microphone, or your location. iOS shows a permission prompt first. If you allow it, only that website can use it, and only while you are using DuoBrowser. You can change these permissions later in the iOS Settings app. DuoBrowser does not access the camera, microphone, or location for itself.

Private browsing

Private browsing uses a temporary cookie store for that screen and does not add pages to History. Leaving private browsing discards those cookies. It does not make you anonymous to the websites you open.

Children

DuoBrowser is a general-purpose browser with unrestricted access to the web. It is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted at this address and shown in the app under Settings, Privacy Policy.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the support page linked from the App Store listing for DuoBrowser.
